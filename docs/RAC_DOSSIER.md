# RAC dossier — George Mason University Recreation and Athletic Complex

Fairfax campus. Compiled for builders and reviewers of the 1:1 Roblox recreation. Every fact has a **source** and a **confidence** (high / medium / low). The walkthrough in `docs/WALKTHROUGH.md` still wins on circulation and lived layout.

**No pool in this building.** Swimming is at the Aquatic & Fitness Center (AFC). Do not invent a natatorium.

- Cardio gallery
- Martial arts / dance room (fee)
- Yoga / Pilates room (fee)
- **4 basketball / 6 volleyball courts**
- **6 pickleball / 6 badminton courts**
- **1 racquetball / wallyball court** (reserve 48 h)
- **2 squash courts** (reserve 48 h)
- Equipment checkout
- Men’s and women’s locker rooms
- **1,500-seat** multipurpose venue for varsity volleyball and wrestling

GoMason / Athletic Business / 2009 opening (slightly older, still useful):

- **Three gymnasiums**
- **Two racquetball + two squash** (2009 count)
- Two-story fitness gallery **> 15,000 sf**
- Juice bar / lounge adjacent to fitness
- RAC Gym: **1,550 seats**, three basketball courts, retractable bleachers, sound system, **second-level viewing**
- **John Linn Memorial Gymnasium** renovated for student use
- **Additional gymnasium on the second level** = Cage Gym
- Home of volleyball, wrestling, intramurals, club sports, **ROTC**, classroom/offices for School of Recreation, Health and Tourism (now Tourism and Events / related units)

**Court-count reconciliation:** 4 BB / 6 VB is overlay math across RAC Gym + Linn (and historically Cage). RAC Gym itself is described as **three basketball courts** that overlay volleyball/wrestling. Linn is the multi-court rec gym (IMLeagues: Linn Court 1/2/3, 1A/1B, badminton 1–6, pickleball in Linn). Confidence high that RAC Gym is the competition box and Linn is the rec box.

**Racquetball vs squash:** 2009 sources say **2 + 2**. Current Mason Rec says **1 racquetball + 2 squash**. 2026 Recreation “facility enhancement” page: **“Racquetball conversion to multi-functional training space.”** Walkthrough: two **racquetball** courts on L2 (user: the old “squash” mention was these). **Build two 40×20 racquetball courts on L2** per walkthrough; optional second squash if photos show glass-back international squash. Confidence high for two enclosed racket courts on L2; medium for which sport they currently are.

**Pool:** none. AFC has competition + recreation pools. Open Rec page lists swimming only at AFC. Confidence **high**.

**Juice bar:** 2009 program, adjacent to fitness. May be reduced; still a lounge/vending zone in the glazed rec hall (walkthrough: ping-pong + vending). Confidence medium that a branded juice bar still operates.

**Classroom / ROTC / multi-purpose:** tour video (2016) shows a classroom (ROTC + health/tourism) and two multi-purpose rooms (yoga/pilates; martial arts/Zumba/kickboxing). IMLeagues: Classroom, Large Conference Room, Martial Arts Room, Yoga/Pilates, Erg Room, Strength and Conditioning.

**Athletic training:** RAC ATR for volleyball and wrestling (GoMason sports medicine). Walkthrough: green treatment tables off the athletic corridor. Nutrition vestibule + industrial fridge are **fictional in-game** (walkthrough).

**Offices:** rec admin + coaches suite (walkthrough: glass entrance, cubicles, private offices, “HEAD COACH” plate — fictional name). Basketball staff still primarily at EagleBank until BAPC.

## 13. Implications for our build

Corrections and fills against `docs/WALKTHROUGH.md`, `docs/SPEC.md`, `blueprint/`, `docs/ARCHITECT_NOTES.md`, `blueprint/HEIGHTS.md`.

1. **Architect of the 2009 RAC is EwingCole, not Perkins&Will.** P&W is only the Cage/BAPC addition. Drawings labeled Perkins&Will in `reference/photos/Screenshot_*` are the addition, not the 2009 plans.

2. **Three named gyms, not two.** Competition = RAC Gym (volleyball/wrestling, 1,550 seats). South/rec = **Linn**. Upper basketball = **Cage** (~15k sf, two courts since 2017). Blueprint already has `competition_gym`, `south_gym`, `cage_gym` — keep all three. Signage should say LINN / RAC GYM / BASKETBALL OFF LIMITS, not generic “South Gym” on the player-facing door if we can spare a decal.

3. **No pool.** Confirmed. Do not add wet leisure beyond locker showers.

4. **Fitness split is canonical:** selectorized + free weights **L1** immediately behind the desk; cardio **L2** on the gallery with a **horizontal wood-cap rail** (tour/Into Mason), not only glass. Walkthrough already has this; prop pass should not dump treadmills on L1.

5. **Racket courts:** user says two **racquetball** on L2, left when walking away from the entrance after the VB overlook. Official rec still advertises 2 squash + 1 RB and is converting racquetball to training in 2026. **Follow the walkthrough** (40×20, L2 only). Ceiling 16.5 ft under a 36.7 ft roof is physically a two-story volume with an intermediate lid or a short court — flag for a photo check; primer default for RB is ~20 ft clear. Do not put racket courts on L1.

6. **Floor-to-floor 20 ft** (walkthrough + HEIGHTS.md) beats the 16 ft validator. PSU **45 ft** building height aligns with Cage roof 45.8 and two-story wings ~33 ft in HEIGHTS.md. Lidar ~25 ft roofs cannot cover a 20 ft floor + ceiling — HEIGHTS.md already resolved this; do not lower L2.

7. **Seating capacity:** 1,500–1,556 in RAC Gym. Current bleacher props must look like a **full retractable bank**, gold/green, not a few rows. Nested depth ~3–4 ft when closed.

8. **BAPC / construction zone:** 28–30k sf addition + 14k reno at **northwest Cage**. Construction start slipped into **summer 2026**, still active through 2027. Keep fencing, gate, unfinished interior. Do not build the academic center or two new practice courts as finished space.

9. **Activities Building (2026)** sits behind the RAC. If the site model grows, it is a **separate** two-gym box, not a RAC wing.

10. **Roof construction differs by era:** 1972 Linn/Cage = **tectum + lightweight fill + BUR**. 2009 RAC Gym / additions = steel joists + metal deck + EPDM. Model exposed **steel joists in RAC Gym** (photos); Cage/Linn may look heavier/older.

11. **Entrance:** walkthrough correction (small south glass + black canopy) matches 2023 pastandpresent SE photo and WEB_entrance_*. Address is Banister Creek Court, not a University Drive storefront.

12. **Juice bar is real (2016): Freshens** in the double-height lounge with ping-pong and mezzanine glass. Walkthrough still has ping-pong + vending. Model a juice-bar counter in the glazed rec hall unless a later photo shows it gone.

17. **Entry doors:** 2016 tour shows a **revolving door plus a paired glass leaf**, not only a pair. Check IMG_0364–0368 before changing the blueprint opening.

18. **Bleacher color:** video = **dark green seats, gold/yellow aisle stripes**. SPEC already says gold/green telescopic. Do not use generic grey.

19. **L2 gym glass:** club VB shows a large **end-wall** viewing window into a brick-lined room, plus clerestory on the long wall. Walkthrough overlook is the long side of competition gym. Both can exist (end vs side). Photo-match before deleting one.

13. **Court markings:** Summer 2025 floor is **new Mason green + vinyl GM logo + natural wood**. Photos IMG_0332+ may predate that; 2025 facilities article is the current look.

14. **MEP visible:** Lithonia 2×4s in ACT rooms; high-bays in joist bays; cooling tower historically SW corner (thesis). Include tower/yard if the service court is in the site model (photos 0349–0351 dumpsters + fenced ramp).

15. **OSM 392 × 341 ft** vs 116–120k sf: 392×341 ≈ 133k footprint including courts/overhangs; two stories over part of that is consistent with 116–120k GSF. Do not inflate the plan to 200k.

16. **Unknowns to ask the user, not invent:** which of the two L2 racket courts is still squash glass-back; whether the juice bar counter still exists; exact Linn court count visible from the hall; whether martial arts / yoga rooms are on L1 or L2 in the 2009 addition.
