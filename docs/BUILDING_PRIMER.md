# Building primer — university rec / athletics gyms like the RAC

Practical numbers for filling gaps in a 1:1 recreation of a 1970s PE building renovated ~2009 (CMU, brick veneer, metal panel, long-span gyms). **When a drawing or photo is silent, use the bold default.** Codes: IBC (typical 2003–2018 / Virginia USBC), ICC A117.1 / ADA, NCAA, SJI 100, SDI, ASTM F2115 / F2440, NFPA 13/72. This is not a substitute for as-builts.

The GMU RAC 2007–09 EwingCole / Gilbane job is the type example. PSU AE thesis (Brian D’Antonio, 2009) on **this building**: Type IB, 2 stories, **45 ft** actual height, A-3/A-4 occupancy, W18×35 typical beams, W10×33 typical columns, 2 in 18 ga deck + 4½ in NWC composite slabs, 1½ and 3 in galvanized roof deck, EPDM roof on the addition, brick veneer + CMU backup on the new venue gym, metal panels + curtain wall + storefront + translucent panels on other elevations. Use photos over this when they conflict.

## 1. Structure — grids, joists, deck

**When unsure: 30 ft × 30 ft office grid; gym 30 ft girder bay × 80–120 ft joist span.**

| Item | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Office / locker / corridor grid | **25–30 ft** (range 20–30) | 8 in CMU module; 5 ft curtain-wall mullions |
| Gym long span | **80–120 ft** clear | RAC competition gym is in this band |
| Joist series | **LH** for gym roofs | SJI: K 10–30 in, spans to 60 ft; **LH 18–48 in, to 96 ft**; DLH 52–120 in, to 240 ft |
| Gym joist depth | **32–48 in** LH (36LH / 40LH / 48LH) | Exposed, painted white |
| Joist spacing | **4–6 ft o.c.**, often **5'-0"** | 1½ in B-deck 20 ga spans ~5–8 ft |
| Joist girders | **20–48 in** JG, panel points at joist spacing | Seats ~7½ in |
| K seats / LH seats | K **2½ in**; LH **5 in** (02–17) or **7½ in** (18–25) | |
| Bridging | Horizontal ≤ 60 ft; **bolted diagonal** if span **> 60 ft** | SJI |
| Roof deck | **1½ in Type B, 20 ga** typical; **3 in Type N** if joists 8–12 ft o.c. | RAC thesis: mix of 1½ and 3 in |
| Upper floor | **2 in 18 ga** deck + **4½ in** NWC (RAC thesis) | Composite |
| Foundations (RAC thesis) | ~**2 ft** strip footing perimeter; spread footings at columns | Braced steel frame |
| Roof membrane | EPDM or TPO on rigid insulation | RAC addition: EPDM (VE from BUR). Original Linn/Cage: multi-ply asphaltic on perlite + 4 in polyiso on lightweight fill on **tectum** deck (2025 roof IFB) |

**Default gym roof: 36LH–48LH @ 5'-0" o.c. on joist girders, 1½ in B-deck, white.**

## 5. Stairs and railings (IBC / ADA)

**When unsure: 7 in rise, 11 in run, 42 in guards, 34–38 in handrails, 4 in sphere.**

| Item | Number | Code |
|---|---|---|
| Riser | 4 in min – **7 in max**, uniform | IBC 1011 |
| Tread | **11 in min** nosing to nosing | IBC |
| Width | **44 in** typical (36 in if occupant load < 50) | IBC |
| Landing | **≥ stair width**; max 12 ft rise between | IBC |
| Headroom | **6'-8" min** | IBC |
| Handrail | **34–38 in** above nosings; 1¼–2 in diameter; 1½ in wall gap | IBC 1014 / ADA |
| Guard | **42 in** commercial | IBC 1015 |
| Openings | **4 in sphere**; 6 in sphere in tread–riser–rail triangle | IBC |
| Glass guards | Tempered/laminated + 42 in top rail or structural glass (IBC 2407) | RAC balcony: horizontal rails ~42 in |
| Intermediate HR | Stairs **> 60 in** wide | IBC |
| Ramp | **1:12 max**, 30 in max rise per run, 60 in landings | ADA |

Default rec stair: steel pan + concrete fill, 7/11, painted CMU, wall HR both sides, 42 in guard at open sides.

## Quick default kit

1. Grid 30 × 30 offices; gym 30 × ~100 joist span.
2. Gym roof 40LH @ 5'-0", 1½ in B-deck, white.
3. F2F 14'-0" offices; gym 30+ ft clear; RAC occupied wings **20 ft F2F**.
4. Walls 8 in CMU paint inside; brick cavity or IMP outside.
5. Doors 3'-0" × 7'-0" HM; gym pairs with panic.
6. Stairs 7/11, 42 in guard, 34–38 in HR.
7. Court 94 × 50, 2 in lines, 10 ft runoff, 6 ft pads.
8. Bleachers 10.5 × 22 in, nested ~4 ft for ~6 rows.
9. Lockers 12–15 × 18 in deep, 18 in benches.
10. Site 9 × 18, 24 aisle, 6 in curb, 5 ft walk, 1:12 ramp.
