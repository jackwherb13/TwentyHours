# RAC furnishing and finish inventory

Photo-by-photo records, measured colors, and merged room counts are in `data/inventory.json`. This file is the readable index. Counts are the minimum visible in the best frame of a room. Overlapping photos of the same object are not added together. Compass headings are `?` unless a facade is identified. Personal names on signs are omitted.

Hex values are the median of a patch on `reference/photos/*.jpg` (see `colorSamples` in the JSON). They are the photographed color, including the room light, not a paint-chip code. Shaded white surfaces read darker than a lit white surface.

Ceiling heights are visual estimates. A 7 ft door under a normal grid is called 10 ft in corridors and 9 ft in the offices and locker room. The competition-gym height is scaled from the green pad band. Nothing here was measured with a tape.

### Competition gym — level 1, joist soffit about 30 ft

Photos: IMG_0332–IMG_0336 and IMG_0337 frames f000–f009. IMG_0344 and IMG_0345 look down into this room from level 2.

Maple (`#D2BA9D`) with a dark green border (`#1C3020`, glossy, so the patch includes reflections), white lines, a center GM-style logo, and **PATRIOTS** on the green. Two telescopic bleacher banks, dark green (`#1E231F`) with gold bays (`#A06C20`); one close frame shows about 14–16 nested rows. Three retractable ceiling hoops are distinguishable. Two volleyball nets, one referee stand. Green wall pads (`#1B201C`) on the photographed walls, about 6 ft, matching the nearby doors. Two scoreboards (HOME / GUEST). Banners: NCAA Women's Volleyball (1993, 1994, 1995, 1996, 2002, 2003, 2009), CAA Women's Volleyball Champions (1992, 1993, 1994, 1995, 1996, 2002, 2009), one retired-jersey banner (number 8, 1985–1988; the name is omitted), plus one high banner in IMG_0332. One US flag. Double doors under the viewing windows. Linear LED high-bays on white steel joists, on the order of twenty in a single ceiling view. Clerestory windows. Step ladders in several frames are temporary.

The 30 ft figure treats the pad band as about one fifth of the floor-to-joist wall in IMG_0336 and IMG_0337_f000. A reasonable range is 28–34 ft.

- Cage Gym interior. Only the entry doors (IMG_0348).
- Basketball gym interior beyond the corridor doors and one window. No full court, no bleachers, no storage.
- Competition gym: the baseline under the far basket is thin, joist spacing was not measured, and scoreboard digits were not read.

- Public men's locker room. Sign only (IMG_0372).
- Women's locker room. No photo.
- Restrooms other than one corner of the volleyball locker restroom.
- Shower room beyond the two stalls.
- Break room 1122 and every private office. Doors only.
- Behind the front desk, and the balcony as a place to stand (the rail is seen from below; IMG_0343 is the upper corridor).
- Level 2 other than the balcony corridor and the gym-view corridor. No level-2 offices, locker rooms, or fitness.
- Level 3, if the building has one. These photos do not show it.
- Elevator cab, mechanical rooms, electrical rooms, janitor, storage.
- Racquetball courts. Not in the walk-through. Four small courts appear only on the addition life-safety plan.
- RAC Addition interior and the northwest construction zone. Drawings only. No close photo of construction fencing around the Cage expansion.
- Roof, the north elevation, and a full south-versus-west assignment for every brick wall.
- A full stair from level 1 to level 2 with a counted riser. IMG_0341 and IMG_0371 are partial flights.
- Night, after-hours, and most artificial-light-only views. These photos are daytime.
- Water fountains and bottle fillers. None identified.
